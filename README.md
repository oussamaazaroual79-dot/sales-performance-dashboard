# Sales Performance Dashboard – Excel

![Dashboard](Sales_Dashboard.png)

## Objective
Track 12 months of sales performance (Oct 2025 – Sep 2026) on a single interactive page, with revenue compared against target and broken down by region, category and sales rep.

## Features
- 6 KPI cards: Revenue, Gross Profit, Profit Margin, Orders, Average Order Value, % vs Target
- Monthly revenue vs target chart
- Revenue by region chart
- Revenue mix by category (Hardware, Software, Services, Accessories)
- Top sales reps by revenue
- Sales rep leaderboard with target attainment (conditional formatting)
- Regional summary table: revenue, profit, margin, vs target
- Region selector (dropdown): KPIs and charts update automatically
- Workbook structure: Dashboard / Data / Calc / Guide

## Tools
Excel (formulas, data validation, dynamic charts, conditional formatting)

## Key Results
- Total revenue: $1,421,593
- Gross profit: $806,836 (56.8% margin)
- 374 orders, average order value $3,801
- 96.2% of annual target achieved

## Key Insights
- West ($384,890) and North ($374,637) are the top regions; East is the weakest ($309,097).
- Sofia Rossi is the top-performing sales rep across all regions.
- Revenue peaked in September 2026, the strongest month of the period.
- Several months (Oct 25, Jan 26, Feb 26) fell well below target, explaining the 3.8% gap to the annual goal.
